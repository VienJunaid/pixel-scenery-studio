# Lantern Lake

The example scene that ships with the studio. It shows a finished brief and a finished
`scene.mjs` side by side.

## The place

An imagined mountain lake. Snowy peaks across the water, a dark pine tree line on the far shore,
a grassy bank in the bottom-left corner and reeds in the bottom right. The focus is the small boat
just left of centre.

## Time, light and weather

Clear night, full moon high on the right. The moon lights the peaks from the upper left as usual,
and lays a glittering path on the water. The boat's lantern is the one warm light.

## Characters

None.

## What moves

- Stars twinkle; one shooting star crosses the upper left once per loop.
- The water ripples: the reflection slides a little, more near the viewer.
- The moon's glitter path flickers.
- The boat bobs; its lantern flickers and lights the water.
- Fireflies drift and blink over the bank.
- Reeds sway.

## Reference images

None (drawn from imagination).
